Wei Chen is an Associate Professor of Operations and Information Management and Director of AI Transformation at the University of Connecticut School of Business. He previously served for three years as Academic Director of the UConn Digital Frontiers Initiative. His research examines how organizations design and govern generative AI capabilities to transform decision-making and knowledge work. His work has appeared in leading journals including *Management Science*, *Information Systems Research*, *MIS Quarterly*, and *Production and Operations Management*. He has received multiple best paper awards and nominations, as well as major early-career honors from the INFORMS Information Systems Society. His current work spans two connected projects: [The T-W-O Project](https://thetwoproject.com), which hosts [The Owner](https://thetwoproject.com/owner.html), a business novel about work in the GenAI era, and *Generative AI for Business: Frameworks, Techniques, and Governance*, a widely adopted textbook supported by [GenAI4All.org](https://genai4all.org/), whose teaching resources have been shared with faculty internationally.
