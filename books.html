The Owner

A business novel about work in the GenAI era.

The Owner lives within The T-W-O Project, the public home of the Task-Workflow-Organization framework. The project site brings together the framework, the novel, related essays, and research organized around AI transformation.

The novel explores what happens when organizations move beyond AI demos and confront the harder questions of workflow redesign, ownership, and capability reallocation.

For the latest preview chapters, project updates, and framework materials, visit the project site directly.

Generative AI for Business

Frameworks, Techniques, and Governance

This textbook is supported by genai4all.org, which serves as the book's public platform for readers, instructors, and course participants. It covers the core mechanisms of large language models, prompt engineering, retrieval augmented generation (RAG), agentic systems, fine-tuning, and the governance of GenAI deployment.

The curriculum balances conceptual foundations with hands-on applications. It also supports the OPIM 5515 graduate course and the Executive Workshop on GenAI described on this site.

Published October 2025.
