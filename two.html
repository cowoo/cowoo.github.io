The T-W-O Framework

Task-Workflow-Organization for AI transformation

The Full Project Lives at The T-W-O Project

T-W-O (Task–Workflow–Organization) is a framework for understanding AI transformation across three connected layers: discrete tasks, the workflows that connect them, and the organizational structures that make redesign stick. The main public home of the framework is thetwoproject.com.

Visit thetwoproject.com

Why T-W-O Matters

Many AI strategies jump between task-level experiments and organization-level ambition without redesigning the workflow in between. T-W-O focuses on that missing middle: transformation compounds only when organizations rethink how tasks connect, how exceptions are handled, and who owns the new operating model.

The Three Levels

  • Task: What should be done by a human, an AI system, or an external provider?
  • Workflow: How should tasks connect so gains compound instead of creating new friction?
  • Organization: Who owns the redesign, incentives, governance, and operating model?

The framework is paired with The Owner, a business novel that explores these ideas in narrative form.
